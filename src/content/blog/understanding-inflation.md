Inflation is often described as "prices going up," but it's more precise to say the *value of money* is going down. That distinction changes how you think about it.

## The usual suspects

- **Demand-pull** — too much money chasing too few goods.
- **Cost-push** — input costs (energy, labor, supply chains) rise.
- **Expectations** — when people expect inflation, they act in ways that create it.

## Why it matters to you

Inflation is a quiet redistribution. It punishes savers and rewards borrowers, and it hits people with fixed incomes hardest. Understanding it is the first step to protecting against it.
