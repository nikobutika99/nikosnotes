Sport is a laboratory for discipline. The scoreboard is honest, the feedback is immediate, and there are no shortcuts that survive contact with a real opponent.

## Lessons that transfer

- **Consistency beats intensity.** The athlete who trains every day outpaces the one who trains heroically once a week.
- **Recovery is part of the work.** Progress happens in the rest between efforts.
- **Process over outcome.** You can't control the result, only the inputs.

The same logic applies to writing, investing, and building anything that takes years.
