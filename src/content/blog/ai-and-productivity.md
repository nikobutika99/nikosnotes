AI doesn't replace thinking — it removes the friction between a thought and a result. The people getting the most from it aren't prompt wizards; they've redesigned their workflow around fast iteration.

## Where it compounds

- **Drafting** — get from blank page to rough draft in minutes.
- **Research** — summarize, compare, and pressure-test ideas.
- **Building** — prototype faster than you can spec.

## The mindset shift

Treat the model as a tireless collaborator that needs clear context and honest feedback. The bottleneck moves from *doing* to *deciding what's worth doing*.
