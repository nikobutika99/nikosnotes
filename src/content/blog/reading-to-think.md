We don't read to collect facts. We read to borrow other minds — to think thoughts we couldn't have reached alone.

## A few principles

- **Read across disciplines.** The best ideas come from connecting distant fields.
- **Reread the great books.** A book that changed you at 18 says something different at 30.
- **Keep a commonplace.** Write down what strikes you; the act of writing is where understanding forms.

## Why it belongs here

Finance, technology, and energy are ultimately about people and ideas. Literature is where we practice understanding both.
