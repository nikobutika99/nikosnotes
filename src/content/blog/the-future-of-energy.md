The energy transition is the defining infrastructure project of our era. Solar and wind are now the cheapest sources of new electricity across most of the world, and the curve is still bending downward.

## Why this decade matters

Three forces are compounding at once:

- **Cost** — solar module prices have fallen by roughly 90% since 2010.
- **Storage** — batteries are following the same learning curve, unlocking firm, dispatchable renewable power.
- **Policy** — capital is flowing toward decarbonization at a scale we've never seen.

> The countries that win the energy transition won't just be cleaner — they'll be more competitive.

## What I'm watching

Grid-scale storage, green hydrogen for heavy industry, and the geopolitics of critical minerals. The transition is not only technical; it's economic and political. That intersection is exactly what Nikosnotes is about.
